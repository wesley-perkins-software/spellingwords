### Hear the vowel teams in out and day

The words *out* and *day* each contain a familiar vowel team. Use **ou** inside *out* and **ay** at the end of *day*. The word *so* has a long o sound too, but it needs only the single **o** at the end.

### Build the short-vowel words around their endings

The words *up* and *had* map directly with short vowels. In *next*, listen through the final blend **st**; in *then*, begin with the digraph **th**. The word *well* ends with **ll**, a useful detail that separates its spelling from a single-l ending.

Several other words in this set have one vowel spelling that deserves focused attention. Their notes pinpoint that part while keeping the consonants available for sound-by-sound mapping.
