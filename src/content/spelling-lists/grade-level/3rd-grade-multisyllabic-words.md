A multisyllabic word has more than one syllable. Third graders are ready to spell longer words by breaking them into smaller spoken and written parts instead of trying to memorize the whole word at once.

Some words on this list have two syllables, like *basket* and *because*. Others have three or more syllables, like *afternoon*, *attention*, and *information*. This list bridges earlier phonics work and the longer academic words students will meet in fourth grade.
