A first-grade writer encounters these words in every kind of text — directions, stories, questions, and answers. Words like *front*, *part*, and *road* don't follow a single tidy pattern, which is why practicing them directly pays off.

Spelling them confidently helps a first grader spend less energy on the small connecting words and more energy on expressing ideas.
