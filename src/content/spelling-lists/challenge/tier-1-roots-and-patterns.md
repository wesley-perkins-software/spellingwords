Many long or unfamiliar words are built from small pieces called roots — each root carries a meaning that travels into every word it touches.

This list focuses on four Latin roots: *port* (carry), *dict* (say or speak), *spect* (look or see), and *rupt* (break). When you can spot and spell the root inside a word, the rest of the spelling follows naturally — and you start to see the same root showing up in words you haven't even studied yet.
