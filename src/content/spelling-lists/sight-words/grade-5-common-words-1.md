Start Grade 5 with twelve words that keep a silent or hidden letter even though the sound has dropped out of speech. *Foreign* keeps its full *eig* even though only one vowel sound survives, and *rhythm* has no normal vowel letter at all.

This set continues the Grade 4 Common Words sequence with words that need to be remembered rather than sounded out — the kind of classic hard spellings a fifth grader is ready to own before middle school.
