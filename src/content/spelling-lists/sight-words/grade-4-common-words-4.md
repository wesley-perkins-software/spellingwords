This last set finishes Grade 4 Common Words with words fourth graders need for longer, more organized writing: linking words that show contrast, addition, or concession (*however*, *furthermore*, *regardless*), and words that are easy to confuse with an earlier Common Word — *altogether* is one word, unlike Grade 2's *together*; *though* is not *thought*; and *weather* is not *whether*.

*Persuade* and *reason* round out the set for the reasons-and-evidence language fourth-grade opinion writing calls for.
