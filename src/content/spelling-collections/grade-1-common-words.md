These six cumulative sets build useful Grade 1 writing words without treating them as a list to memorize blindly. Children use familiar phonics when possible and learn only the unexpected spelling parts by heart.

The first two published sets contain 24 words. They are a good fit for first graders who are using short-vowel, long-vowel, and early spelling-pattern knowledge while learning a few irregular portions by heart.
