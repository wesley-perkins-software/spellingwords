Fourth grade introduces Greek and Latin roots as word families. In fifth grade, students use those familiar word parts inside longer academic words. The Greek parts in *photography*, *biology*, *geography*, and *telescope* help with meaning, while Latin parts in *transportation*, *construction*, *inspection*, and *description* help students see how spelling and meaning travel together.

This list is not a new root course from the beginning. It is practice using word parts to spell and understand more challenging Grade 5 vocabulary.
