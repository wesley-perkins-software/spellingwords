The short "o" sound — the round, open vowel in hot and dog — is the third stop in the kindergarten vowel sequence. As with the earlier lists, every word here keeps the same simple consonant-vowel-consonant shape, changing only the vowel sound in the middle.

By this point in the sequence, a child has practiced isolating three different vowel sounds one at a time. That repetition matters more than any single list — it is what makes the vowel sound itself, not just the individual words, start to feel automatic.
