These twelve words are grouped into three **word families** — cat/hat/bat/mat share the -at ending, can/man/tan/van share -an, and bag/wag/tag/rag share -ag. Every word in a family rhymes, and every word is spelled the same way after the first letter changes.

That's the real skill this list builds: once a child can spell "cat," spelling "hat" and "bat" is mostly a matter of swapping the first letter — the same move a speller reuses for every new -at word they meet later, in this list or anywhere else.

All twelve words use the short-a vowel sound, so this is a good page to slow down on before moving to a different vowel. Short-i CVC Words is a natural next step once these three families feel comfortable.
