EE and EA are two of the most common ways to spell the long-E sound in English, and students encounter them in nearly every reading passage from second grade onward. Words like *sleep*, *clean*, and *need* appear constantly — in stories, in directions, in science texts.

Unlike the AI/AY pattern, there is no single rule for choosing EE over EA. The best strategy is wide reading and repeated practice with both patterns, which is exactly what this list is designed for.
