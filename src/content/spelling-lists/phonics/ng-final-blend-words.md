A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In NG words, the /n/ and /g/ sounds blend at the very end, making the soft closing heard in *sing*, *king*, and *long*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the NG blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *sang* and *thing* to longer words like *strong* and *young*. Recognizing NG as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
