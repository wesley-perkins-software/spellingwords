A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In NT words, the /n/ and /t/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *ant*, *tent*, and *front*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the NT blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *ant* and *hint* to longer words like *plant*, *spent*, and *front*. Recognizing NT as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
