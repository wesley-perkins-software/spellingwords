Fifth grade math turns to fractions and decimals in earnest, and these eight words are the vocabulary that comes with it — the parts of a *fraction* (*numerator*, *denominator*), and the words for comparing amounts (*equivalent*, *percent*, *ratio*).

This is optional additional practice, not part of the Grade 5 Core Spelling route. *Denominator* and *hundredths* are the two longest, least phonetically obvious words on this page.
