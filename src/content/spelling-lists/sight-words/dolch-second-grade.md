Dolch Second Grade words build on the first-grade tier, gathering the next set of high-frequency words a student is expected to read and spell automatically. These words appear constantly in second-grade stories and classroom writing, and many of them — like *their*, *which*, and *would* — have tricky spellings that can't be solved by sounding out alone.

Recognizing these words on sight frees a young reader's attention for meaning and expression, making reading smoother and writing more confident.
