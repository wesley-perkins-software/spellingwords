Telling time is one of Grade 3's key math skills, and this page gathers the words that go with it — from the units themselves (*hour*, *minute*, *second*) to the kind of *clock* a child is reading (*digital* or *analog*).

This is optional additional practice, not part of the Grade 3 Core Spelling route. *Elapsed* is the trickiest word here — it names the exact math concept ("how much time has passed") a third grader is learning alongside telling time to the minute.
