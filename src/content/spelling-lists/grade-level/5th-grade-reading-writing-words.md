Fifth-grade writers are expected to plan, draft, revise, and argue. This list names the pieces they work with: the *argument* they build, the *consequence* they explain, the *conclusion* they reach, the *information* they gather. *Writing* is the process; *written* is the product — both words appear constantly in assignments, prompts, and teacher feedback.

Words like *connection*, *section*, and *solution* share the -tion ending that fifth graders encounter across every subject, making them worth spelling carefully and confidently. Spelling them automatically keeps a fifth grader's attention on the ideas, not the letters.
