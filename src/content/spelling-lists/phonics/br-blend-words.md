In BR words, the /b/ and /r/ sounds blend together at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *bring*, *brown*, and *bright*.

The nine words in this list give children practice with the BR blend across common, useful words — including tricky past-tense forms like *broke*, *broken*, and *brought*. Building familiarity with BR as a unit helps children recognize and spell it reliably in everyday reading and writing.
