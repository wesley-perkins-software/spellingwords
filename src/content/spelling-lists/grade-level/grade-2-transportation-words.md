### Compare the vowel teams

The long *a* sound in *train* is spelled *ai*, while the long *o* sound in *boat* is spelled *oa*. At the end of *subway*, *ay* spells the long *a* sound. Keeping each vowel team with its whole word helps distinguish these three spellings.

### Build two words from smaller words

The compounds *airplane* and *subway* can be built without changing their smaller words: *air + plane* and *sub + way*. Spell each part, then join the parts into one word.

### Use sounds and chunks for the rest

The short words *car*, *bus*, and *truck* can be mapped sound by sound; *car* also has the r-controlled spelling *ar*. For the longer word *bicycle*, hold the three written parts *bi*, *cy*, and *cle* in order.
