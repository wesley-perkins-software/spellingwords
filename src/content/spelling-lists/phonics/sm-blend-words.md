In SM words, the /s/ and /m/ sounds blend together at the very start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they move into one another smoothly. You can hear it clearly in *small*, *smell*, and *smile*.

The six words in this list give children practice with the SM blend across familiar, useful words — from describing words like *small* and *smooth* to action words like *smell* and *smile*. Spotting SM as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this blend wherever they find it.
