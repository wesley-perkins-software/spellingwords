Start Grade 4 with twelve words that keep a silent or unexpected letter even after the sound has dropped or shifted in speech. *Environment* keeps both n's even though the first one is barely said, and *island* keeps a silent s that most speakers never pronounce.

This set continues the Grade 3 Common Words sequence with words that need to be remembered rather than sounded out. Use the Heart Word hint to notice the one part of each word that does not match how it sounds.
