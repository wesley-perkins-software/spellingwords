A prefix is a meaningful word part added to the beginning of a base word. Third graders are ready to move beyond reading short words by sound and begin using word parts to spell and understand longer words.

The prefix *un-* often means "not" or "reverse," as in *unkind* and *unlock*. The prefix *re-* often means "again," as in *reread* and *rewrite*. The prefix *pre-* means "before," while *dis-* and *mis-* can show an opposite meaning or a wrong action. Learning these prefixes helps students spell longer words by finding the base word first.
