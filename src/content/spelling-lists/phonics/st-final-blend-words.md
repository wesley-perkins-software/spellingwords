A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In these ST words, the /s/ and /t/ sounds blend at the very end, making the crisp closing heard in *best*, *last*, and *nest*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the final ST blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *just* and *rest* to words like *fast* and *west*. Recognizing ST as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
