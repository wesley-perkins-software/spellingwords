This list stays with the same word shape as Short A Words — one consonant, one vowel, one consonant — but changes the vowel in the middle to short "i," the crisp sound in pig and sit.

Practicing one vowel sound in isolation, rather than several at once, helps a child build a clear, separate mental "box" for each sound before they start telling vowels apart from one another. That skill — hearing which vowel is in the middle of a word — is exactly what makes the later Mixed Vowel CVC Review meaningful practice instead of a guessing game.
