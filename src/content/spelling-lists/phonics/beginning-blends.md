A beginning consonant blend is two consonant sounds sitting side by side at the start of a word, with each sound still heard. *Black* keeps both the /b/ and the /l/; *stop* keeps both the /s/ and the /t/. This Skill gathers common beginning blends — including bl, cl, gr, fl, st, and sw — so children practice hearing and writing both letters, not treating the pair as one new sound.
