Once students know the silent-e rule from long-A words, the same pattern immediately unlocks long-I words. A student who can spell *cake* already has the tools to spell *bike* — the only thing that changes is the vowel in the middle.

Working through long-I words reinforces that the silent-e rule is a pattern, not a special case. These ten words give students repeated practice hearing the difference between the short and long vowel, spelling the final *e*, and building the confidence to apply the rule to words they haven't seen before.
