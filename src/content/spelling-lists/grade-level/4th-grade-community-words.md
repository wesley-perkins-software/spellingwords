Fourth graders are making sense of the world around them — learning who lives nearby, who serves the community, and how people work together. A *neighbor* waves hello; a *volunteer* gives their time; a *doctor* cares for the sick; a *captain* guides the way. *Community* and *citizen* are the words that hold it all together.

Spelling these words precisely gives fourth graders the tools they need to write confidently about social studies topics, local events, and the everyday connections between people.
