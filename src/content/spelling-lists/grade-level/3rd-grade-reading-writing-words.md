Third-grade readers and writers use these words constantly — in book discussions, comprehension questions, writing assignments, and classroom directions. A student who can spell *reason*, *believe*, and *decide* has an easier time writing about what they think and why.

These are not flashy words, but they are the ones that show up every single school day, in every subject.
