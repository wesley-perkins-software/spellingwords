### Compare three two-letter vowel spellings

The words *new*, *way*, and *now* each use a vowel pair, but each needs a different one: **ew** ends *new*, **ay** ends *way*, and **ow** ends *now*. Saying the letter pair while spelling helps keep these three endings distinct.

### Use familiar frames and endings

The words *him* and *them* share the final **m** and use short-vowel spellings before it. The words *from* and *will* also have familiar consonant frames; remember that *will* finishes with **ll**.

In *time*, silent **e** makes the **i** say its name. The word *our* is the complete letter sequence found at the end of *your*, although the two words may not sound exactly alike. The remaining spellings that need individual attention are identified in the notes.
