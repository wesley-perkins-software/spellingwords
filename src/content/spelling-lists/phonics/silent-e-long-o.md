Long-O words complete the core silent-e sequence. By this point, students have seen the same rule at work with three different vowels — long A, long I, and now long O — which is exactly when a spelling pattern starts to feel automatic.

Words like *hope*, *home*, and *stone* are among the most common in early reading, so the phonics practice here pays off immediately in fluency and writing. From here, the next step is vowel teams — a new way of spelling the same long vowel sounds.
