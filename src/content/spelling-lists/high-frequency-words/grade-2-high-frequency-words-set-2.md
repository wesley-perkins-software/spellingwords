### Compare vowel teams

The words *school* and *book* both contain **oo**, but the team spells two different vowel sounds. In *school*, **oo** has the sound heard in *room*; in *book*, it has the sound heard in *good*. The word *around* uses **ou** for a different vowel sound and can be held in the chunks **a** and **round**.

The words *because* and *been* also contain two-letter vowel spellings. Their notes identify the exact teams and written chunks to keep, rather than treating either whole word as unpredictable.

### Build from consonant patterns and endings

The words *best*, *both*, and *which* can be mapped with familiar consonant patterns: the final blend **st**, the digraph **th**, and the opening **wh** and ending **ch**. In *little*, the stable **le** ending follows a doubled **t**. Other words in the set have a silent letter or a less expected vowel spelling, so their notes pinpoint the part to retrieve.
