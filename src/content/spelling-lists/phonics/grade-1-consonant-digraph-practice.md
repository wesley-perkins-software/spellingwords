## What this practice family covers

Focused SH, CH, TH, and WH practice for students who need extra work with two letters spelling one consonant sound. This page is a gateway to smaller, targeted Level 3 practice pages so the Grade 1 hub can stay focused on the curriculum roadmap.

## When to use this page

Use this page after students have encountered the related core lesson: [Consonant Digraphs & Final -ck](/spelling-lists/phonics/1st-grade-consonant-digraphs-final-ck). It is especially helpful for review, intervention groups, homeschool practice, or a teacher assigning one narrow pattern.

## How parents can practice at home

Pick one targeted page, practice five to eight words, and ask your child to explain the spelling pattern before typing or writing each word. Keep the focus narrow: one pattern at a time is better than a long mixed list.
