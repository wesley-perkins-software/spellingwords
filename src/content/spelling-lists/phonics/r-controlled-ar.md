R-controlled vowels introduce a new concept: when R follows a vowel, it changes the vowel's sound entirely. The AR in *car* sounds nothing like the short A in *cat* or the long A in *cake* — the R takes over. Students who have mastered short vowels and long vowels are ready to meet this third case.

The AR pattern is one of the most common r-controlled vowels in English, appearing in everyday words like *farm*, *park*, and *dark*. Practicing these ten words builds the pattern recognition that makes reading AR words — and spelling them — automatic.
