Long **e** can be spelled more than one way. This list helps second graders compare **ee** and **ea** in familiar words without asking them to discover a rule that does not exist.

The list groups **ee** words such as *tree, feet,* and *sleep* before **ea** words such as *eat, team,* and *beach*. Children learn to keep both vowel letters and use a familiar word as a spelling anchor. The linked Skill page explains these patterns in more depth.

## How to practice this list

Sort the words under **ee** or **ea**, then dictate a mixed set. Ask your child to underline the vowel team and compare an uncertain spelling with a known word such as *keep* or *team*.

A child may write only one vowel or choose the other long-**e** team. Calmly confirm that the sound is right, show the correct anchor word, and invite the child to revise the vowel team rather than rewrite the whole word.

Move on when your child can spell the words in mixed order, keep both vowel letters, and use a familiar example to correct an occasional **ee/ea** mix-up.
