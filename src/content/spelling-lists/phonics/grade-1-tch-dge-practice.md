## What this practice family covers

Focused short-vowel ending practice for final -tch and -dge words. This page is a gateway to smaller, targeted Level 3 practice pages so the Grade 1 hub can stay focused on the curriculum roadmap.

## When to use this page

Use this page after students have encountered the related core lesson: [Short Vowel Ending Rules: -tch and -dge](/1st-grade/tch-dge-ending-rules). It is especially helpful for review, intervention groups, homeschool practice, or a teacher assigning one narrow pattern.

## Sample words

- catch
- match
- pitch
- ditch
- badge
- edge
- bridge
- fudge

## Choose a targeted practice page

- [TCH and DGE Ending Words](/spelling-lists/phonics/tch-dge-ending-words)

## How parents can practice at home

Pick one targeted page, practice five to eight words, and ask your child to explain the spelling pattern before typing or writing each word. Keep the focus narrow: one pattern at a time is better than a long mixed list.
