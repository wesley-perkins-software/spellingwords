Dolch Third Grade words complete the core Dolch sight word sequence, bringing together the high-frequency words a fluent third-grade reader encounters every day. Many of these words have irregular or unexpected spellings — *laugh*, *eight*, *carry* — making them perfect candidates for sight-word practice rather than phonics-only study.

Mastering this tier means a student has covered the full foundation of the Dolch word program, giving them an automatic command of the most common words in English reading and writing.
