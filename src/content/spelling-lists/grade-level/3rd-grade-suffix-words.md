A suffix is a meaningful word part added to the end of a base word. In third grade, students begin using suffixes to compare, describe, and make new words for their own writing.

The suffixes *-er* and *-est* compare, as in *faster* and *fastest*. The suffix *-ly* often tells how something happens, as in *slowly*. The endings *-ful* and *-less* change meaning in words like *helpful* and *careless*. The suffixes *-ness* and *-ment* can turn a familiar word into a noun, as in *kindness* and *movement*. Some suffixes also require spelling changes, which third graders practice in the next rule lists.
