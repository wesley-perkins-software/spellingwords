A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In NK words, the /n/ and /k/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *bank*, *pink*, and *think*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the NK blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *sink* and *wink* to words with more letters like *skunk*, *trunk*, and *drink*. Recognizing NK as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
