Start Grade 2 with the words that hold sentences together. Most sound out with patterns your child knows. *Enough* and *through* share the same surprising letters — learn that `ough` part once and it covers both.

This phonics-aware set uses regular sound-spelling patterns where they help. When a small part is not yet predictable, use the Heart Word hint as a reminder to learn that part by heart.
