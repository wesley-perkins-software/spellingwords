**Instructional variant** and **Content brief status** (added per `docs/content/CANONICAL_SKILL_PAGE_STANDARD.md` §21) are distinct from **Editorial status**: Editorial status tracks the roadmap's page-level definition of done (§8 of the roadmap); Content brief status tracks only whether a Skill's content brief (`docs/content/templates/SKILL_PAGE_CONTENT_BRIEF.md`) has been written and approved, using `Not started`, `Drafting`, `Needs review`, `Approved`. ## Resolved: legacy-role exception — pages tagged `contentRole: skill` outside the frozen taxonomy

These two published, grade-owned pages are **settled as not canonical Skills**: they are not, and will not become, Browse-by-Skill destinations, and they are correctly excluded from the 41-slot taxonomy and from `CANONICAL_SKILL_PAGE_STANDARD.md`'s Skill mapping. That question is closed — it is not tracked as unresolved.

What remains open is narrower and purely technical: both pages still carry their pre-PR legacy `contentRole: skill` frontmatter value. A prior attempt to introduce a third `contentRole` for pages like these was rejected and rolled back, and reclassifying them to `contentRole: grade-unit` was evaluated and found to have side effects beyond a metadata fix — it would route them through the `GradeUnitWorldPage` template and the cross-grade world-kit sequencing in `src/lib/content/gradeUnitSequence.ts` (see that file and `src/pages/spelling-lists/[category]/[slug].astro`), changing their live public rendering. Rather than accept that unrelated behavior change, or invent a fourth `contentRole` value, `contentRole: skill` is being **kept deliberately, as a documented legacy-role exception**: the one available frontmatter value that currently produces their correct, intended public behavior (a plain content page, reachable only via their anchor Grade Unit's related-practice link, not listed on the Skills Hub or as its own Grade Hub card). This is a conscious retention, not an oversight.

Both pages are grade-owned focused component pages of an existing combined Grade Hub unit — see the "Focused component page" notes for `grade-4-final-stable-syllables` and `grade-5-spelling-rules` in `grade-curriculum-pages.md` for their curriculum placement and disambiguation from neighboring canonical Skills. They are not new standalone Grade Hub units and do not change any Grade Hub card count.
