Fourth-grade readers and writers meet longer words in every subject. Many of these words are not tied to one topic; they help students explain ideas, organize information, and understand directions in science, social studies, and ELA.

Students can spell these words more confidently by breaking them into syllables and meaningful parts before moving into advanced prefixes, suffixes, and roots.
