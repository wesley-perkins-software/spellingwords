### Use the shared *er* ending

*Teacher*, *farmer*, and *baker* all end in *er*. Their beginnings preserve useful spellings from *teach*, *farm*, and *bake*: the vowel team *ea*, the r-controlled *ar*, and the silent-e spelling in *bake* before the final *e* is dropped to add *er*.

### Build the compound *firefighter*

Spell *firefighter* by joining *fire + fighter*. Inside *fighter*, the word *fight* keeps its *igh* spelling before *er* is added. Building the two meaningful parts keeps all eleven letters in order.

### Break longer words into syllables

Use spoken and written parts for *doc-tor*, *den-tist*, and *li-brar-i-an*. The endings differ: *doctor* ends in *tor*, *dentist* ends in *tist*, and *librarian* ends in *ian*. The short word *nurse* has the r-controlled spelling *ur* and a final silent *e*.
