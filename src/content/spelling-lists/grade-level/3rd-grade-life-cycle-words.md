From a butterfly's *egg* and *caterpillar* stage to a frog's *tadpole* stage, life cycle units are a classic part of elementary science. These eight words name the stages and structures a third grader learns to describe in order.

This is optional additional practice, not part of the Grade 3 Core Spelling route. *Caterpillar* is the longest word on this page — a good one to sound out syllable by syllable.
