Vowel teams introduce a new concept: instead of one vowel doing the work with a silent helper at the end, two vowels work together in the middle or at the end of a syllable. The AI and AY teams both spell the long-A sound — but with a reliable rule: AI appears in the middle of a word, AY at the end.

Students who already know the silent-e rule for long A will recognize a familiar sound in a new costume. These twelve words cover both patterns, giving students practice hearing the sound and choosing the right spelling for each position in a word.
