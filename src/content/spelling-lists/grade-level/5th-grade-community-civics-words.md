By fifth grade, students read and write about how communities, governments, and people shape history. These words help them discuss what it means to be a *citizen*, how a *democracy* works, why an *election* matters, how a *law* affects people, and why *independence* and *rights* matter in civic life.

Spelling these words without hesitation lets a fifth grader focus on the ideas behind social studies and civics.
