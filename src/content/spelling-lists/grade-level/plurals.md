Plural -s and -es are endings added to nouns to change meaning from one to more than one. Most words simply add -s (*dogs*, *cups*), while words ending in a hissing or buzzing sound — *x*, *sh*, *ch*, *s*, or *z* — add -es instead (*boxes*, *dishes*, *buzzes*), because saying the extra syllable makes the plural clear.
