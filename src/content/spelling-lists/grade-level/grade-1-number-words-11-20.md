### Seven words share the ending *teen*

The words *thirteen*, *fourteen*, *fifteen*, *sixteen*, *seventeen*, *eighteen*, and *nineteen* all end in *teen*. Spell this stable ending with the vowel team *ee* each time.

### Look for a familiar number word at the beginning

The beginnings of *fourteen*, *sixteen*, *seventeen*, *eighteen*, and *nineteen* keep the spellings of *four*, *six*, *seven*, *eight*, and *nine*. In *thirteen* and *fifteen*, the beginnings *thir* and *fif* change from the spellings of *three* and *five*, so compare them carefully.

### Organize the words outside the *teen* group

The words *eleven*, *twelve*, and *twenty* do not use the *teen* ending. Say each word in two spoken parts, then check its beginning, middle vowel, and ending rather than trying to attach the shared pattern.
