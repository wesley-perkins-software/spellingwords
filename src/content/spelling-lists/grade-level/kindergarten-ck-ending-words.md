Every word on this list ends in **-ck**, the spelling used for a final /k/ sound right after a short vowel in a one-syllable word. It's a spelling-choice rule, not a new sound: a child already knows how /k/ sounds, this list is about knowing which letters to write.

This is the last required page in kindergarten's core phonics sequence. After the five short vowels and two small rule-based extensions (qu- and -ck), a child has everything needed to decode and spell the large majority of simple, regular words.

From here, Kindergarten Animal Words and Kindergarten Number and Color Words are optional, high-interest supplemental lists available anytime — and Grade 1 picks up the phonics sequence with consonant blends, digraphs, and more advanced spelling rules.
