By fourth grade, students are expected to write structured responses, cite evidence, compare ideas, and discuss what an author does and why. These words are the language of close reading and clear writing.

Spelling them automatically lets a fourth grader stay focused on the thinking behind an answer instead of the mechanics of putting each word in order.
