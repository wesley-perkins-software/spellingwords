Verbs are the engine of every sentence, and a first-grade writer reaches for these twelve constantly — in journal entries, story retelling, and morning messages. Words like *write*, *catch*, and *build* have tricky spellings that are worth practicing directly.

Knowing how to spell the actions in a story lets a young writer stay focused on the story itself.
