# Frozen K–5 High-Frequency Words Curriculum

**Status:** Approved and frozen — August 2026

**Executable contract:** `src/lib/content/hfWordsCurriculum.ts`

This document is the canonical human-readable record of the 27-set, 316-word High-Frequency Words curriculum. When older planning or implementation-history text conflicts with this inventory, this document and the executable contract govern.

## Ownership principles

- **Frequency** determines eligibility for High-Frequency Words.
- **Spelling complexity and developmental appropriateness** determine the grade of conventional spelling ownership.
- Core Spelling teaches spelling structures and patterns; High-Frequency Words develops accurate and increasingly automatic retrieval. A word may legitimately appear in both.
- Themed Additional Practice may overlap when a word independently belongs in both purposes.
- HFW ownership itself is unique: 316 assignments equal 316 normalized unique spellings.
- The smaller Grade 4–5 inventories are intentional and must not be padded with lower-frequency academic vocabulary.
