A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In ND words, the /n/ and /d/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *hand*, *pond*, and *kind*.

The nine words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the ND blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *end* and *sand* to long vowel patterns like *find*, *kind*, and *mind*. Recognizing ND as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
