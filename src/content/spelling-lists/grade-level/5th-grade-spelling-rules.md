Some fifth-grade spelling patterns appear again and again in academic words. Endings like *-tion* and *-sion* can sound alike, while *-able* and *-ible* or *-ance* and *-ence* require careful attention to word families and memory.

This list keeps those patterns together so students can practice useful rules without turning spelling into a long list of exceptions.
