These twelve words form three word families built on the short-o sound: dog/jog/log/fog share the -og ending, hot/pot/dot/cot share -ot, and mop/top/hop/pop share -op. As with the earlier vowel lists, every word in a family rhymes and is spelled the same way after its first letter.

Short-o comes third in the sequence — after short-a and short-i are secure — because it's phonetically distinct enough to add without much confusion, while still following the standard a-i-o-u-e order used in most kindergarten phonics programs.

Once these three families feel solid, Short-u CVC Words is the next stop in the vowel-by-vowel sequence.
