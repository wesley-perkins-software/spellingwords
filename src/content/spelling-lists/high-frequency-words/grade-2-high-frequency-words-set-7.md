### Sort the long-vowel spellings

Long e appears as **ee** in *free* and as **ea** in one pronunciation of *read*. The words *few* and *show* end with different teams, **ew** and **ow**, while *white* uses silent **e** and *night* uses **igh** for long i. Keeping each sound tied to its written team helps prevent the spellings from blending together.

### Anchor spellings that can sound different

The written forms of *read* and *live* do not change when their pronunciations change; their notes show what remains stable. In *water*, pronunciation varies across accents, but the spelling stays **wa** + **ter**. The words *same* and *white* follow familiar silent-e patterns, while *set* provides a straightforward short-vowel contrast. In *house*, keep **ou** between the opening **h** and the ending **se**.
