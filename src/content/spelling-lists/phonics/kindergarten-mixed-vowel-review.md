**Short Vowels and CVC Words** is Kindergarten's core phonics milestone: spelling simple, one-syllable consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC) words by hearing each short vowel sound clearly and matching it to a letter. Short A, Short I, Short O, Short U, and Short E each build one vowel sound at a time; this page is the checkpoint, not a new lesson, that ties them together.

These ten fresh words mix all five vowel sounds using words a child has not seen on any earlier list. The goal is to notice whether the child can hear the vowel sound and spell it in a brand-new word — the same skill that will let them spell words they have never practiced at all, long after this list is behind them. If a single vowel still needs more focused practice, the five single-vowel lists linked below remain available any time.
