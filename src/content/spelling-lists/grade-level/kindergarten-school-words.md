By kindergarten, most children already spend a good part of their day surrounded by school vocabulary — their *backpack*, their *pencil*, their *teacher*, their *friend*. These ten words describe the classroom world a kindergartener lives in five days a week.

Learning to spell these words connects a child's everyday school routine to their early writing, making practice feel immediately useful rather than abstract.
