The CL blend puts /k/ and /l/ together at the start of a word — both sounds are there, flowing smoothly into each other. You can hear it in the crisp opening of *clap*, *clock*, and *clean*.

The nine words in this list give children practice with CL across a range of familiar words — from action words like *clap* to everyday nouns like *clock* and *clothes*. Recognizing CL as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this pattern wherever it appears.
