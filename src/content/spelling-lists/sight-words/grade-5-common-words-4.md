This last set finishes Grade 5 Common Words with words fifth graders need for longer, more organized writing: precise linking words that show cause, contrast, or emphasis (*consequently*, *particularly*, *despite*), and the reference and editing vocabulary — *punctuation*, *apostrophe*, *vocabulary*, *thesaurus* — that goes with proofreading and revising a piece of writing.

Together with the connecting words, this set closes the K–5 Common Words sequence on the editing habits fifth graders will keep using in middle school.
