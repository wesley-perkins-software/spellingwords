These are the words children reach for when they write about something that already happened. Sound out the regular ones, then notice that *does* and *goes* look alike but sound different, and that *been* and *done* each hide one unexpected letter.

This phonics-aware set uses regular sound-spelling patterns where they help. When a small part is not yet predictable, use the Heart Word hint as a reminder to learn that part by heart.
