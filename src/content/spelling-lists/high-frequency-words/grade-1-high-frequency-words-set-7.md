### Connect long vowels to their spellings

The words *place* and *life* use silent **e** to help the earlier vowel say its name. The words *keep* and *room* use vowel teams instead: **ee** spells the long e sound in *keep*, while **oo** spells the vowel sound in *room*. These four words show two different ways a spelling can hold a long vowel sound.

### Hold on to consonant patterns

The words *part* and *hard* share **ar**, with a different two-letter ending after it. In *thing*, the digraph **th** begins the word and **ng** finishes it. The words *end*, *left*, *man*, and *top* use short or familiar vowel sounds; listening all the way through their final consonants keeps each spelling complete.

The spelling of *oh* includes a final letter that is not heard, so its note gives that letter special attention.
