Every word on this list answers the same question the same way: after a short vowel, how do you spell the /k/ sound at the end of a one-syllable word? The answer, every time, is -ck — not a single k on its own.

This is the first rule-based spelling pattern in the kindergarten sequence, rather than a simple one-letter-one-sound match. Learning it here, with a small and consistent set of words, sets up the fuller c/k/ck spelling choice that Grade 2 covers in much more depth.
