A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In SK words, the /s/ and /k/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *ask*, *desk*, and *mask*.

The seven words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the SK blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short, common words like *ask* and *risk* to words like *desk*, *dusk*, and *tusk*. Recognizing SK as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
