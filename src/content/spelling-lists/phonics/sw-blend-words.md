In SW words, the /s/ and /w/ sounds blend together at the very start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they move into one another smoothly. You can hear it clearly in *swim*, *swing*, and *sweet*.

The six words in this list give children practice with the SW blend across familiar, useful words — from everyday action words like *swim* and *sweep* to describing words like *sweet* and objects like *swing*. Spotting SW as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this blend wherever they find it.
