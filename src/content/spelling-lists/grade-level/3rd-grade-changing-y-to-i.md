When a base word ends with a consonant plus *y*, the *y* often changes to *i* before an ending. That is why *try* becomes *tried*, *city* becomes *cities*, and *happy* becomes *happier* or *happiness*.

This rule helps third graders spell plurals, past-tense verbs, comparatives, superlatives, and noun suffixes. Students should first find the base word, then decide whether the final *y* needs to change before the ending is added.
