When fifth graders write to persuade or respond to a prompt, they reach for these ten words. A well-built *argument* begins with a clear *claim*, backs it up with *evidence*, and considers the *consequence* of each idea. Writers *explain* their thinking, *respond* to different views, and *summarize* their reasoning in a strong *conclusion*.

Knowing how to spell these words quickly means a student can spend their energy on the ideas — not the mechanics.
