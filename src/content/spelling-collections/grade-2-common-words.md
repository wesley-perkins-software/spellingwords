These six sets carry a second grader through the last heavy year of common-word learning. Most of these words can now be sounded out with the patterns your child already knows — vowel teams, syllables, and silent letters. Where one small part is still unexpected, the page names it so your child learns only that part by heart.

Together, the six sets contain 72 words. They are a good fit for second graders who are building on short-vowel, long-vowel, vowel-team, and early spelling-pattern knowledge while learning a few irregular portions by heart.
