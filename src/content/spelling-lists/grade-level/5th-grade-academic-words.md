By fifth grade, teachers expect students to do more than summarize — they ask for analysis, evidence, and reasoned responses. This list collects the verbs and nouns at the center of that work: *claim* what you think, *compare* two examples, explain the *cause* and *effect*, *support* your reasoning, and *respond* to what you've read.

Words like *achieve*, *purpose*, and *result* appear in assignment prompts, rubrics, and feedback comments as often as they do in student writing. Spelling them automatically lets a fifth grader stay focused on the thinking, not the mechanics.
