The OI and OY vowel teams both produce a diphthong — a sound that starts in one place and glides to another. Like the AI/AY and OA/OW patterns, there's a positional rule: OI appears in the middle of a word, OY at the end.

These ten words give students practice with both spellings, including some that students use in everyday speech (*joy*, *voice*, *noise*) and some that appear in early science reading (*soil*, *oil*).
