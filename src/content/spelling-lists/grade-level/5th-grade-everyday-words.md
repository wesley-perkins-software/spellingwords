By fifth grade, a student meets words that carry real weight in sentences — words that connect ideas (*although*, *except*), describe what characters do (*explore*, *arrive*), and express how things change (*improve*, *introduce*). These twelve words appear constantly in fifth-grade reading and writing, across fiction, nonfiction, and every subject.

Spelling them without thinking lets a fifth grader stay focused on the meaning of what they're writing, not the mechanics.
