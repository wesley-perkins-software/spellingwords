In FL words, the /f/ and /l/ sounds blend together at the very start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they move into one another smoothly. You can hear it clearly in *flag*, *flip*, and *fly*.

The eight words in this list give children practice with the FL blend across familiar, useful words — from short action words like *flap* and *flip* to everyday nouns like *floor* and *flower*. Spotting FL as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this blend wherever they find it.
