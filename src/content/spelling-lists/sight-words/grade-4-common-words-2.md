This set groups words with a doubled consonant that isn't triggered by adding a suffix to a base word — unlike Grade 3's doubling-final-consonants spelling rule, these doubled letters just have to be remembered. *Necessary* famously has one c and two s's; *embarrass* has both doubled letters at once.

Say each word slowly and picture the doubled letter before writing it. These words show up often in explanations, opinions, and longer stories.
