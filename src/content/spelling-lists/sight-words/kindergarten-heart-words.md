Most words in this spelling progression can be fully sounded out, letter by letter. Heart words are different: most of each word is regular, but one small part is not — so that part gets learned by heart instead.

Say each word slowly and notice which parts match their sounds and which one does not. *Said*, for example, starts and ends exactly the way it sounds — only the middle needs to be remembered. That small amount of memorizing, paired with the sounding-out skill built across the rest of this progression, is what lets a child read and spell the words that show up on almost every page of an early book.
