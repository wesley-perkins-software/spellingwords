Number and color words are things a kindergartener is asked to read and write constantly. Spelling *seven* correctly — with an e in the middle — or remembering the silent gh in *eight* can trip up even careful young writers.

This is a **supplemental** list, like Kindergarten Animal Words — a closed, high-frequency set worth spelling well, but not a phonics pattern, so it doesn't gate progress through the core short-vowel and phonics-rule sequence. Practicing these words builds the kind of automatic recognition that lets a child focus on what they're writing, not how to spell the color of the sky.
