### Begin every day name with a capital letter

The first letter is part of each correct written spelling: *Sunday*, *Monday*, *Tuesday*, *Wednesday*, *Thursday*, *Friday*, and *Saturday* all begin with a capital letter.

### Keep the ending *day* stable

Every word in this set ends with the same three letters, *day*. After spelling the different beginning, add *day* and check that its vowel is written with *a*.

### Learn the different beginnings

The beginnings *Sun*, *Mon*, and *Fri* are fairly easy to hear. In the longer names, keep the written chunks *Tues*, *Wednes*, *Thurs*, and *Satur* before adding *day*. Comparing these chunks helps prevent missing letters in the middle.
