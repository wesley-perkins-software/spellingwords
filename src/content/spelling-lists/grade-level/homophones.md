Homophones sound alike but carry different spellings and meanings. *To*, *too*, and *two* often need the most repeated practice because all three sound identical; *there*, *their*, and *they're* are easier once each spelling is tied to a clear meaning. The strategy is the same for every pair: decide what the word means in the sentence first, then choose the spelling that matches.
