# Canonical Grades Gateway Standard

**Canonical route:** `/grades`  
**Status:** Frozen for launch  
**Decision date:** 2026-08-23

## Purpose and editorial ownership

The Grades page answers: **What specifically changes from Kindergarten through 5th Grade?** It owns the cross-grade comparative progression. Its content must help an adult understand adjacent-grade development and must be grounded in the canonical curriculum.

## Required structure

The page contains a concise K–5 orientation followed by six distinct, sequential grade chapters. Each chapter names the grade, explains its curriculum fingerprint, states the change from the preceding stage, and provides exactly one primary curriculum link: its canonical Grade page beneath `/grades/{grade}`.

The page may briefly orient readers to Core Spelling, High-Frequency Words, and Themed Spelling Practice. It must not reproduce complete strand or unit inventories, become six interchangeable cards, or link every unit.

## Ownership boundaries

- The homepage remains the product-discovery switchboard and retains six direct grade links plus one quiet progression link to `/grades`.
- `/curriculum` owns educational philosophy, the three-strand architecture, Skills relationships, and only a high-level account of systematic progression.
- `/grades` owns the complete six-grade comparison and adjacent-grade distinctions.
- Each Grade page owns detailed one-grade orientation and routing to its three strands.
- Grade–strand pages own complete inventories for one strand within one grade.
- Top-level strand pages own cross-grade browsing of one strand.
- Skills remain grade-independent concept references.

## Content and navigation quality

Descriptions must be specific enough to remain identifiable without their headings, readable in dark normal-sized text, and accurate to frozen route and curriculum data. Grade placement describes curriculum organization rather than a rigid learner limit. The visual treatment must communicate a connected progression using Direction A and must avoid a generic card grid or childish rainbow treatment.

No canonical grade curriculum route may exist outside `/grades`. Because this is a pre-launch change, old root-grade routes receive neither aliases nor redirects.
