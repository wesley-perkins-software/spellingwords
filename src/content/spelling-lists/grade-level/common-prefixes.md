A prefix gives a learner a stable chunk to spell before focusing on the base word. *Dis-* and *mis-* build on the earlier un-/re- pattern with two more common meaning-changing prefixes. *Pre-* means "before," as in *predict*. Upper-elementary academic words add *inter-* ("between"), *sub-* ("under"), *super-* ("above"), and *trans-* ("across"), which appear constantly in longer school vocabulary like *interact*, *submarine*, and *transport*.
