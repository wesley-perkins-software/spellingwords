### Start with the sounds you can hear

*Red* has three letters for its three sounds. In *black*, listen for both *b* and *l* at the start and keep *ck* together at the end. In *pink*, listen all the way through the ending *nk*.

### Notice the vowel chunks in four words

The middle of *green* uses *ee*. *Blue* ends in *ue*, and *brown* uses *ow*. In *white*, the final *e* works with *i* to spell the long *i* sound. Remembering each vowel chunk reduces how many letters you need to work out one at a time.
