A second-grade writer needs action words that go beyond simple movement — words like *follow*, *learn*, and *watch* that describe what people do in stories, in school, and in everyday life. These twelve verbs appear constantly in second-grade reading passages and student writing alike.

Spelling them automatically frees a young writer to focus on what they want to say, not on how to spell the word that says it.
