### Link words through stable spelling chunks

The words *high* and *light* share **igh** for the long i sound; *light* adds **l** before that team and **t** after it. In *only*, the opening word *on* remains visible, followed by **ly**. The familiar endings **er** in *other* and *together* can also help preserve their final letters.

### Keep the vowel teams distinct

Several long vowel sounds use different teams in this set: **ay** ends *say*, while **ee** ends *three*. The **oo** in *took* spells the same vowel sound heard in *book*, not the one in *room*. *Came* follows the silent-e pattern, and *kind* uses **i** for its long sound before **nd**. The notes focus on vowels and chunks that are less clear from pronunciation alone.
