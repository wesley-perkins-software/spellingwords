"Community helpers" is a familiar classroom unit, and these eight words name the people a second grader already recognizes from their own neighborhood and school. *Firefighter* and *librarian* are the two longest words on this page — a good spot to slow down and sound out each part.

This is optional additional practice, not part of the Grade 2 Core Spelling route.
