When R follows the letter O, it changes the vowel's sound entirely — the OR in *corn* sounds nothing like the short O in *cot* or the long O in *coat*. This is called an r-controlled vowel, and the OR pattern is one of the most common in everyday English.

The ten words in this list appear regularly in early reading and writing. Practicing them helps children recognize the OR pattern quickly, so spelling words like *morning*, *short*, and *order* becomes natural and confident.
