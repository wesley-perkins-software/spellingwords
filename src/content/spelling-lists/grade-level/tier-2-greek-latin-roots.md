Greek roots appear in many words fourth graders meet in reading, science, and social studies. Roots such as *tele* meaning far, *photo* meaning light, *graph* meaning write, *bio* meaning life, *demo* meaning people, and *scope* meaning look help students connect spelling with meaning.

This list follows the Latin roots list and keeps Grade 4 focused on useful morphology instead of separate narrow topic lists.
