Meals and snacks are part of a kindergartener's daily rhythm, and these ten words are the food vocabulary that comes up again and again — at breakfast, at lunch, and in stories about hungry characters and shared meals.

From simple staples like *bread* and *milk* to everyday favorites like *banana* and *cereal*, this list gives a young speller the vocabulary to write about what they eat and share at the table.
