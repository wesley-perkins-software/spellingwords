In PR words, the /p/ and /r/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *press*, *pretty*, and *prize*.

The eight words in this list give children practice with the PR blend across common, everyday words — from simple words like *pray* and *print* to more familiar words like *pretty* and *present*. Recognizing PR as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
