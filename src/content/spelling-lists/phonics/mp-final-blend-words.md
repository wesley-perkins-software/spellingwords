A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In MP words, the /m/ and /p/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *bump*, *camp*, and *stamp*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the MP blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *bump* and *dump* to words like *camp*, *ramp*, and *stamp*. Recognizing MP as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
