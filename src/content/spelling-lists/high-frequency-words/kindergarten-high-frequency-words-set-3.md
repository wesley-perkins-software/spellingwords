### Listen for the two-letter consonant teams

The beginning of *she* uses **sh** for one sound. The words *this* and *that* begin with **th**. After that first letter team, listen through the rest of each short word: **e** in *she*, short **i** in *this*, and short **a** followed by **t** in *that*.

### Long vowel sounds can look different

The long vowel at the end of *she* is spelled with **e**. In *see*, the long e sound is spelled **ee**. The word *like* uses **i** followed by a final silent **e**. These endings sound long, but they are not written the same way, so match each sound to the spelling used in that word.

Several other words in this set need one part checked rather than guessed. The spelling notes focus on those particular vowel and ending choices.
