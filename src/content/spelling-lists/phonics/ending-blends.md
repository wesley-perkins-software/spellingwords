An ending consonant blend is two consonant sounds sitting side by side at the end of a word, with each sound still heard. *Hand* keeps both the /n/ and the /d/; *best* keeps both the /s/ and the /t/. This Skill gathers common ending blends — including nd, st, nt, and mp — so children practice hearing and writing both final letters instead of dropping one.
