Most nouns become plural by adding *-s*, as in *dog* to *dogs*.

Words ending in *s*, *x*, *sh*, or *ch* usually add *-es*. That is why second graders practice spellings like *buses*, *boxes*, *dishes*, *brushes*, and *lunches* together.
