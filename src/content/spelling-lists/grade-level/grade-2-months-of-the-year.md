### Begin every month with a capital

*January* through *December* are names, so each begins with a capital letter. When checking a spelling, inspect the first letter as well as the letters inside the word.

### Use two shared endings

*January* and *February* both end in the written chunk *uary*. *September*, *October*, *November*, and *December* all end in *ber*. Spell the beginning of each word, then attach the shared ending without changing it.

### Group the shorter month names

*March* ends with the consonant team *ch*. *April* and *August* each have two syllables; *August* begins with the vowel team *Au*. *May*, *June*, and *July* use three different ways to spell their main vowel sounds: *ay* in *May*, final *e* helping *u* in *June*, and final *y* in *July*.
