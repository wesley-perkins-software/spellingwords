In CR words, the /k/ and /r/ sounds blend together right at the start. You can hear both sounds — but they flow into each other quickly, making the crisp opening in *crab*, *crow*, and *crack*.

The eleven words in this list give children practice with the CR blend across everyday words — from short words like *cry* and *crib* to more descriptive words like *crawl* and *creek*. Recognizing CR as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
