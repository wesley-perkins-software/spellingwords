By second grade, writers start describing more than just size and speed — they describe how characters feel, what makes something special, and how a place looks. Words like *brave*, *kind*, and *lovely* appear constantly in the stories second graders read and write.

Spelling these describing words with confidence means students can use them naturally in their own sentences, without slowing down to sound them out.
