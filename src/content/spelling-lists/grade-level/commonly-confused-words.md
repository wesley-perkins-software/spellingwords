Some word pairs are confused for reasons beyond sound. *Affect* and *effect* look similar and are both about causing change; *principal* and *principle* sound alike but mean entirely different things. This Skill asks students to compare meaning and context, not just spelling — the same "does this match the sentence" thinking that homophones require, applied to a wider set of tricky pairs.
