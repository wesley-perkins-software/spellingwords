A third-grade writer reaches for these words constantly — whether they're describing where something happened, explaining a sequence of events, or asking a good question. Words like *during*, *toward*, and *whose* appear in science articles, history passages, and story problems alike.

Spelling them confidently frees students to focus on expressing their ideas rather than pausing to puzzle out how a familiar word is spelled.
