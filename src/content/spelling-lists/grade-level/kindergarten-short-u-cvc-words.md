These twelve words form three word families built on the short-u sound: bug/hug/rug/mug share the -ug ending, sun/run/fun/bun share -un, and cut/but/hut/nut share -ut. As with the earlier lists, every word in a family rhymes and is spelled the same way after its first letter.

Short-u comes fourth in the sequence, ahead of short-e, because it's more clearly distinct from short-i than short-e is — saving the trickiest vowel pair for last gives it the most recent, most reinforced position in a child's memory.

Once these three families feel solid, Short-e CVC Words completes the five-vowel sequence.
