Recognizing that "sunshine" is just "sun" plus "shine" gives children a powerful spelling strategy: break a big word into smaller, familiar pieces. Every word here is a closed compound — the two smaller words are joined with no space and no hyphen, the most common and most predictable compound pattern for a young speller to learn first.
