A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In LT words, the /l/ and /t/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *belt*, *bolt*, and *melt*.

The eight words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the LT blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *belt* and *felt* to words like *bolt*, *jolt*, and *salt*. Recognizing LT as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
