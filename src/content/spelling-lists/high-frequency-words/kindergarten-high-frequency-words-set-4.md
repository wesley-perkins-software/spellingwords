### Build the short-vowel words from sounds

The words *big*, *yes*, *let*, and *got* use familiar short vowels. Say each word slowly, listen from beginning to end, and match each sound to a letter. *Yes* begins with **y**; *let* ends with **t**; and the other sounds are just as useful for building the spellings.

### Letter teams appear in different places

In *with*, the final sound is spelled **th**, with two letters working together. The word *look* has **oo** in the middle, and *play* has **ay** at the end. These pairs do different jobs, so notice where each one belongs instead of treating every two-letter pair the same way.

The words *come*, *two*, and *old* have spelling details that are easy to miss when a child writes only the sounds heard most clearly. Their individual notes identify the letters to keep.
