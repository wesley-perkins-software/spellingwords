Short "e" completes the set of five short-vowel sounds — a, i, o, u, and now e — that a kindergartener has practiced one at a time across this sequence. As always, the word shape stays the same: one consonant, the vowel, one more consonant.

Finishing all five vowels in isolation is not the end goal — it is the setup for the next list. Mixed Vowel CVC Review brings all five sounds together in one set of brand-new words, which is the real test of whether a child can tell the vowels apart, not just spell each one on its own dedicated list.
