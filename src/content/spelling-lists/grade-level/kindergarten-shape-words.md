Shapes are everywhere in a kindergartener's world — the circle of a plate, the square of a window, the triangle of a slice of pizza. Learning to name and spell these words gives children a vocabulary for describing shapes they already see and sort every day.

This list also includes *side* and *corner* — the words a kindergartener uses to talk about a shape's parts, not just its name. Together, these ten words build the geometry vocabulary a child will keep using well beyond kindergarten.
