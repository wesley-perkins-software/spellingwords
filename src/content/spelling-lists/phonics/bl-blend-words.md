A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In BL words, the /b/ and /l/ sounds blend at the very start, making the smooth opening heard in *black*, *blue*, and *blow*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the BL blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from simple three-letter patterns like *blow* to slightly longer words like *blanket* and *blend*. Spotting BL as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
