These twelve words are a natural first step for a young speller: short, familiar, and drawn from the world a kindergartener already knows — the animals they see, the weather outside the window, the objects on the kitchen table.

Each word is simple enough to feel like a win, and common enough to show up again and again in early books and classroom writing.
