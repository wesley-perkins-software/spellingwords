In PL words, the /p/ and /l/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *play*, *plant*, and *please*.

The nine words in this list give children practice with the PL blend across common, everyday words — from short words like *plum* and *plus* to more familiar words like *place* and *please*. Recognizing PL as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
