Every word in this set has at least one unstressed vowel — a vowel said so quickly and quietly in speech that its sound alone doesn't reveal how to spell it. *Separate* and *several* both hide an unstressed vowel in the middle that could plausibly be spelled several other ways.

This set is a natural place to practice what the Grade 4 Core Spelling route calls schwa: an unclear vowel sound that has to be remembered rather than sounded out, the same challenge behind many of Grade 4's longer academic words.
