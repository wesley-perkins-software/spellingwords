A suffix is a word part added to the end of a base word. Some suffixes change meaning: *-ful* means "full of" or "having" (*helpful*), and *-less* means "without" (*careless*). Others compare: *-er* compares two things (*faster*), and *-est* names the greatest degree in a group (*fastest*). This Skill focuses on what each suffix means and how it's used — the base-word spelling changes some suffixes trigger, such as doubling a final consonant, are their own Skill.
