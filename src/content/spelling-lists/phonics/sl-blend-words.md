In SL words, the /s/ and /l/ sounds blend together at the very start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they move into one another smoothly. You can hear it clearly in *sleep*, *slide*, and *slow*.

The seven words in this list give children practice with the SL blend across familiar, useful words — from short action words like *slip* and *slide* to everyday words like *sleep* and *slow*. Spotting SL as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this blend wherever they find it.
