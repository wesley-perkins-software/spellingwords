From the very first days of kindergarten, children talk about their bodies constantly — a bumped *arm*, a stubbed *toe*, a tickle on the *nose*. These twelve words are the parts of the body a kindergartener already points to and names out loud, now paired with the spelling to match.

Learning to spell *hand*, *foot*, and *head* alongside less obvious words like *knee* and *ear* gives a young speller a complete, practical vocabulary — one they will use in stories, songs, and everyday conversation for years to come.
