Fourth graders already practice -tion and -sion in Advanced Suffixes. This short list completes the set of common final stable syllables with two more endings that behave the same way: *-ture*, as in *furniture* and *adventure*, and *-sure*, as in *treasure* and *measure*.

Both endings land on an unstressed final syllable that is easy to spell by sound alone. Naming the ending before writing the word helps students keep the conventional spelling instead of writing what they hear.
