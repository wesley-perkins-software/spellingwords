In ST words, the /s/ and /t/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *star*, *stop*, and *story*.

The ten words in this list give children practice with the ST blend across common, everyday words — from short words like *stop* and *step* to more familiar words like *star*, *stone*, and *story*. Recognizing ST as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
