Use these common people, action, and sentence words to write about yourself and others. Many become easier to explain as children learn long-vowel patterns; to, do, and you still need a remembered part now.

This phonics-aware set uses regular sound-spelling patterns where they help. When a small part is not yet predictable, use the Heart Word hint as a reminder to learn that part by heart.
