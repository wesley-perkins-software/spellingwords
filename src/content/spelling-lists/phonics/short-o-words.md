The short "o" is a round, open sound — you can feel your mouth drop slightly when you say hot or dog. It shows up in many of the concrete, everyday words that children talk about and read about in their earliest books. Recognizing it quickly is a real advantage for young readers.

A few minutes of calm spelling practice with short-o words helps children hear the pattern clearly and spell it with confidence. There is no trick to it — just steady, gentle repetition that makes the sound feel familiar and the spelling feel natural.
