### Most of these words can be built from sounds

In *am*, *at*, *can*, *in*, and *it*, a child can say the word slowly, listen from beginning to end, and match the sounds to familiar letters. The word *and* works the same way, but its final two sounds are close together: stretch the ending just enough to hear both **n** and **d**.

Have the child use these sound clues to build each spelling instead of trying to memorize the whole word as a shape.

### Some words need one extra reminder

The other four words each have one spelling detail that deserves special attention. The individual notes explain what to listen for or remember.
