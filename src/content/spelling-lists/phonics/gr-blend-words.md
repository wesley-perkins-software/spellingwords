In GR words, the /g/ and /r/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *grass*, *green*, and *grow*.

The nine words in this list give children practice with the GR blend across common, everyday words — from short words like *grab* and *grin* to more familiar words like *grass*, *green*, and *ground*. Recognizing GR as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
