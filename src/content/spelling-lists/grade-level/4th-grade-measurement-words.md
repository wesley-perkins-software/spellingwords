Fourth graders learn to compare and convert standard measurement units in math, so it helps to spell the unit names accurately in word problems, recipes, and everyday writing. This list covers the customary units of length (inch, foot, yard, mile), weight (ounce, pound, ton), and liquid volume (cup, pint, quart, gallon).

These words don't belong to the Core Spelling route — they don't share a spelling pattern the way a Grade Unit does. This page is additional practice, useful whenever measurement words come up in class.
