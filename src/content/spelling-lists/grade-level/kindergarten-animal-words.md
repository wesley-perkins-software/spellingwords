Cats, dogs, cows, and horses are often a kindergartener's first favorite words — familiar, easy to picture, and fun to talk about. This list gathers ten common farm and pet animal names that most children already recognize by sight and sound.

This is a **supplemental** list, not part of the core kindergarten phonics sequence — a warm, high-interest set to practice anytime, not a page a child needs to clear before moving on. Some of these words (cat, dog, pig, hen, fox) are simple CVC words that reinforce the short-vowel lists; others (duck, cow, rabbit, frog, goat) are learned as whole words, the same way real kindergarten animal-word worksheets mix the two.

These words are simple, concrete, and rewarding to spell correctly — a great match for a young speller building confidence one short word at a time.
