### Build with recognizable word parts

The compound *someone* joins **some** and **one** without changing either spelling. Several other words can be kept in manageable parts: **un** + **til** in *until*, **al** + **most** in *almost*, and the two spoken syllables in *began* and *never*.

### Notice consonants beside the vowels

The short vowel words *small* and *off* end with doubled consonants. In contrast, *own* and *told* use the vowel spellings **ow** and **o** before their final consonants. Three words need more focused attention: *answer* keeps a silent letter, *large* ends in soft **g**, and *learn* uses the written vowel chunk **ear**.
