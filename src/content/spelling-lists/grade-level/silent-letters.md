Silent-letter words include letters that are written but not pronounced. Three common patterns cover most of what children meet in early reading and writing.

**WR words** begin with a silent *w*: *write* and *wrong*. **KN words** begin with a silent *k*: *know* and *knee*. **MB words** end with a silent *b*: *thumb* and *lamb*.
