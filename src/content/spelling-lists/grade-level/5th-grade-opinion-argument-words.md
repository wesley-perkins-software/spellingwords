When fifth graders write an opinion or argument, they need words that help them reason clearly. A strong *argument* begins with a clear *claim*, gives a *reason*, uses *evidence*, and *explain*s why the ideas matter. Writers also *respond* to other views, use each *transition* carefully, and end with a thoughtful *conclusion*.

Knowing how to spell these words quickly means a student can spend more energy on the ideas and less on the mechanics.
