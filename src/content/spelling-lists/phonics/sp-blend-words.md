In SP words, the /s/ and /p/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *spin*, *spot*, and *spring*.

The eight words in this list give children practice with the SP blend across common, everyday words — from short words like *spin* and *spot* to more familiar words like *space* and *spring*. Recognizing SP as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
