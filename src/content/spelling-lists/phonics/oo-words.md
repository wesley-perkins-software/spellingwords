Second graders already know that one letter can spell more than one sound — short and long vowels have taught them that much. The `oo` vowel team pushes the idea further: the very same two letters spell two different sounds depending on the word. *Moon* and *food* use the long sound; *book*, *look*, and *foot* use a shorter one.
