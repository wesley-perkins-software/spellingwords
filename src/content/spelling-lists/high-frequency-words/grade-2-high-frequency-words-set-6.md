### Map the consonant frames

The words *must* and *went* have short vowels inside familiar beginning and ending consonants. In *think*, **th** begins the word and **nk** ends it; in *much*, **ch** spells the final sound. These clear consonant frames leave the vowels and vowel teams as the main points of comparison across the set.

### Contrast ways to spell the vowels

The word *away* ends in **ay**, *take* uses silent **e**, and *each* uses **ea** for a long e sound. The word *right* holds its long i sound in the **igh** team. *Too* uses **oo**, and its note connects that spelling to meaning so it is not confused with *to* or *two*. The notes also mark the less transparent written vowels in *work* and *done*.
