Short "u" — the quick, low sound in bug and sun — is the fourth vowel a kindergartener practices in isolation. The word shape is unchanged from the earlier lists: one consonant, the vowel, one more consonant.

With four vowel sounds now practiced one at a time, a child is close to being able to compare them directly — noticing not just what each vowel sounds like, but how it differs from the others. That comparison is exactly the skill the next two lists, Short E Words and then Mixed Vowel CVC Review, are designed to build.
