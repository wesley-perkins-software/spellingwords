In TR words, the /t/ and /r/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *tree*, *train*, and *truck*.

The eight words in this list give children practice with the TR blend across common, everyday words — from short words like *try* and *trip* to more familiar words like *train*, *tree*, and *trail*. Recognizing TR as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
