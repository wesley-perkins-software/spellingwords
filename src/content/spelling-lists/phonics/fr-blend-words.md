In FR words, the /f/ and /r/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *frog*, *free*, and *frost*.

The eight words in this list give children practice with the FR blend across common, everyday words — from simple words like *frog* and *from* to more descriptive words like *fresh* and *frost*. Recognizing FR as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
