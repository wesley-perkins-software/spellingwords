Social studies, current events, and everyday conversation all draw on this vocabulary. A fourth grader writing about local government needs *principal* and *police*; writing about family history needs *cousin* and *village*; writing about civics needs *president* and *country*.

Words like *hospital* and *company* also appear in reading passages across subjects, making them worth practicing directly. Spelling them confidently helps students read more fluently and write with greater precision.
