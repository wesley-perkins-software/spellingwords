These four small sets help kindergarteners spell common sight words and everyday writing words used in early reading and writing. Most words can be connected to sounds your child knows; when one small part does not match yet, the page points it out as a Heart Word part.

The sets contain 40 words in all and are designed for kindergarten practice. Work through them in order, using regular sound-spelling patterns where they fit and remembering only the unexpected parts.
