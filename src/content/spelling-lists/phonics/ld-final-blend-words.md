A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In LD words, the /l/ and /d/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *cold*, *hold*, and *wild*.

The ten words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the LD blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from simple patterns like *old* and *fold* to words with long vowels like *bold*, *cold*, and *gold*. Recognizing LD as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
