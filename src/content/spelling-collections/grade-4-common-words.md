These four sets carry a fourth grader through a narrower, more corrective set of writing words than earlier grades: words that don't sound out with a single phonics rule and instead need to be recognized, remembered, or told apart from a confusable partner. Each set groups its words by a shared spelling problem — a silent or unexpected letter, a doubled consonant, an unstressed vowel, or a confusable connecting word — the same way earlier grades' sets group words by shared pattern.

Together, the four sets contain 48 words with zero overlap against the 184 words already owned by the Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, and Grade 3 Common Words sets.
