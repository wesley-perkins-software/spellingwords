### Use known patterns to hold several spellings together

The words *an* and *but* can be built by matching familiar short-vowel sounds to letters. In *after*, the first syllable also uses short **a**. The word *all* uses the familiar double **l** after its vowel, while *or* keeps **r** beside the vowel. These known patterns make most or all of each spelling easier to map.

### Compare the vowel teams

The words *they* and *down* each use a two-letter vowel team, but the teams are different. The long a sound at the end of *they* is spelled **ey**. The vowel sound in *down* is spelled **ow**. Name the team as part of each spelling so the two choices do not get mixed up.

The remaining words have a sound or letter that is less expected. Their spelling notes identify the particular part to learn without treating the whole word as something to memorize by sight.
