In DR words, the /d/ and /r/ sounds blend together right at the start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they flow into one another quickly. You can hear it clearly in *drop*, *drum*, and *dream*.

The ten words in this list give children practice with the DR blend across common, everyday words — from short words like *dry* and *drip* to more familiar words like *dream* and *drive*. Recognizing DR as a unit makes it easier to read and spell this blend wherever it appears.
