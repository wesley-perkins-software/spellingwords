Color words help kindergarteners describe the world around them: a red ball, a blue sky, or a brown rabbit. They appear naturally in art, sorting, stories, and classroom directions, so they are useful words to recognize and spell.

This is optional additional practice, not the core phonics path. Encourage a child to say each color, listen for the sounds they know, and notice any spelling parts that need a little more practice.
