Good writers reach for words like these when they want a reader to see, hear, and feel what they're describing. A path isn't just a path — it's *narrow*. A voice isn't just a voice — it's *loud* or *gentle*. These describing words give a third-grade writer the tools to be specific.

Spelling them automatically means students can use them freely in their own writing, without stopping to sound them out mid-sentence.
