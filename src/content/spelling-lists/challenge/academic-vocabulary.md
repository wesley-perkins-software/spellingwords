These twelve words come up constantly in upper-elementary classrooms — in science investigations, social studies discussions, reading responses, and writing assignments. They are the words teachers use when giving directions and the words students need when explaining their thinking.

Spelling them automatically means one less thing to slow you down when the ideas are flowing.
