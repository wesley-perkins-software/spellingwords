These eleven words travel across every subject a fifth grader studies. In science, students *examine* evidence and record a *result*. In math, they work through a *process* to reach a *solution*. In writing, they *support* a claim and *suggest* a next step. In any subject, they need to *identify* key ideas, gather *information*, and *communicate* what they've learned.

Spelling these words without pausing lets a student stay focused on the thinking — not the letters.
