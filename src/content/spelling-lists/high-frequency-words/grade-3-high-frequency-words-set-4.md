### Build words with recognizable parts

Two words join complete words: *sometimes* is **some + times**, and *nothing* is **no + thing**. These compounds preserve the spellings of their smaller words, providing reliable anchors for the longer spellings.

Five words end in **-ly**, but their bases explain the letters before that ending. *Finally* is **final + ly**, and *really* is **real + ly**, so each has double **l** where the base and ending meet. *Usually* keeps **usual**, and *actually* keeps **actual**, before **-ly**. *Probably* follows a different change, explained in its note.

### Contrast vowel and consonant patterns

The long i sound in *might* uses **igh** before **t**. *Follow* has a more familiar short vowel but needs double **l** before its final **ow**. The notes isolate the less expected vowel spellings in *world* and *believe* and the two spelling surprises in *once*.
