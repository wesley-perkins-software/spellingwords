In SN words, the /s/ and /n/ sounds blend together at the very start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they move into one another smoothly. You can hear it clearly in *snow*, *snake*, and *sniff*.

The eight words in this list give children practice with the SN blend across familiar, useful words — from everyday words like *snow* and *snack* to vivid words like *snail* and *sneak*. Spotting SN as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this blend wherever they find it.
