These four sets carry a fifth grader through a narrower, more corrective set of writing words than earlier grades: words that don't sound out with a single phonics rule and instead need to be recognized, remembered, or told apart from a confusable partner. Each set groups its words by a shared spelling problem — a silent or hidden letter, a doubled letter, an unstressed vowel, or a connecting or editing word — the same way earlier grades' sets group words by shared pattern.

Together, the four sets contain 48 words with zero overlap against the 292 words already owned by the Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 2, Grade 3, and Grade 4 Common Words sets.
