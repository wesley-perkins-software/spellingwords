Longer academic words often lose a whole syllable in casual speech. *Interesting* is usually said with three syllables, not four, and *temperature* drops its middle the same way. Fifth graders need to remember the full spelling even when speech shortens it.

This set continues the unstressed-vowel pattern from Grade 4's Common Words, moving from shorter school words into the longer, more abstract vocabulary fifth-grade narrative and opinion writing calls for.
