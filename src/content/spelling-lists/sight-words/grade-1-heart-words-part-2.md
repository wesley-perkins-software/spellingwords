## Introduction: what students learn

This targeted practice page supports the First Grade Heart Words core unit. Students practice a small set of high-frequency words by mapping the regular parts and noticing the part that must be remembered by heart.

## Learning Objectives

Students will learn to read, spell, and write these heart words with attention to both regular and irregular spellings.

## Parent-friendly explanation

A heart word is not a word to memorize as a whole shape. Say the word, tap the sounds, write the letters that match expected sounds, and mark the unexpected part.

## Practice word list

- have
- give
- come
- some
- done
- does
- goes
- once

## Example sentences

- I can spell **have**.
- We read **give** in a book.
- Write **come** and mark the heart part.

## Common Misconceptions

- **Misconception:** Every letter in a heart word is irregular.  
  **Correction:** Most heart words have regular parts. Only the unexpected part needs special attention.

## How parents can practice at home

Practice two or three words at a time. Ask your child to say the word, map the sounds, write the word, underline the regular parts, and mark the heart part.
