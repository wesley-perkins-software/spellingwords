Describing words are the first step toward richer writing. A kindergartener who can spell *happy* and *funny* can already write a sentence that says something real about the world — not just "the dog ran" but "the funny dog ran fast."

These twelve adjectives are simple enough for a kindergartener to recognize and use naturally, and common enough to appear in every early reader, worksheet, and morning message.
