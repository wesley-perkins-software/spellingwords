A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In LK words, the /l/ and /k/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *milk*, *silk*, and *bulk*. In some LK words like *walk*, *talk*, and *chalk*, the L is silent — children will hear just the /k/ at the end, but the spelling still follows the same LK pattern.

The six words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the LK blend across familiar, everyday words. Recognizing LK as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
