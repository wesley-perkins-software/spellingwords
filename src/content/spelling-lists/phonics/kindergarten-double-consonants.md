Like The -ck Ending Rule before it, this list is built around one specific, learnable pattern rather than sounding a word out letter by letter: in many short, one-syllable words, a short vowel is often followed by a doubled f, l, or s — off, ball, mess.

This is the last spelling rule in the kindergarten phonics sequence. After a child can hear and apply this pattern confidently, the final page — Kindergarten Heart Words — shifts to a different kind of word: a small handful of very common words that do not follow a clean rule at all, and simply need to be learned by heart.
