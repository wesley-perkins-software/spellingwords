Describing words let a first-grade writer show — not just tell — what something is like. A day isn't just a day; it's *warm* or *wet*. A cup isn't just a cup; it's *full*. Practicing these twelve adjectives helps students reach for precise words instead of vague ones.

Spelling them automatically means less stopping mid-sentence, and more time spent on the story itself.
