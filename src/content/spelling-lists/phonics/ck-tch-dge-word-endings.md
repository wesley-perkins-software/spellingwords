Three short-word ending patterns share the same underlying question: what do you write right after a short vowel, at the end of a one-syllable word? *Ck* answers it for the /k/ sound (*back*, *duck*). Doubled consonants answer it for -ll and -ss (*bell*, *mess*). *Tch* and *dge* answer it for the /ch/ and /j/ sounds (*catch*, *badge*). Grouping them together helps children see one pattern instead of three unrelated rules.
