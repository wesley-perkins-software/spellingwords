### Four words share the same ending

The words *he*, *we*, *me*, and *be* all end with **e**. In each word, the final **e** says its letter name. Listen for the first consonant sound, then add **e**.

### Short o helps with two words

The words *on* and *not* use the familiar short **o** sound. Say each word slowly and listen for the consonants around the vowel. In *not*, all three sounds match the letters **n-o-t**.

The remaining words do not all use the same vowel spelling. *Go* ends with **o**, while *my*, *to*, and *you* need the particular vowel reminders in the spelling notes.
