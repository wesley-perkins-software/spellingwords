First Words showed that sounding out works. This step slows down and stays with a single vowel sound — the short "a" heard in the middle of *hat* and *map* — so students can hear it clearly, over and over, before the progression moves to a new vowel.

Every word here is fully regular: a learner who says each sound (h-a-t) and blends it back together will spell it correctly. Practice the eight words above, and when they feel steady, Kindergarten Short I Words is the next step.
