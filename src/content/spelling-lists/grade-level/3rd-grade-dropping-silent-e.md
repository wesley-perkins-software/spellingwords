Many base words end with a silent *e*: *make*, *take*, *come*, *have*, *love*, *write*, and *ride*. When a suffix begins with a vowel, third graders usually drop the silent *e* before adding the suffix.

That is why *make* becomes *making*, *write* becomes *writing*, and *love* becomes *loving*. This rule helps students connect Grade 2 silent-e phonics to Grade 3 morphology: the base word is still there, but the spelling changes before the suffix is added.
