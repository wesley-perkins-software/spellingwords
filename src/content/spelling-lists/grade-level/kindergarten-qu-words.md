Every word on this list starts with **qu**, which always spells the /kw/ sound together — q is almost never used without a following u in English. That makes qu behave like a single consonant unit, not two separate letters to sound out one at a time.

This is a small, narrow addition to a kindergartener's spelling toolkit, not a big leap: once short-vowel CVC words feel secure, learning one new letter-pattern rule is a manageable next step.
