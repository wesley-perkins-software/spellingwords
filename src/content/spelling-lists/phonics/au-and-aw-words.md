The `au` and `aw` spellings both make the vowel sound heard in *saw* and *cause* — a sound that is neither short nor long. Like the vowel teams taught earlier, there is a helpful positional habit to notice: *aw* tends to show up at the end of a word or right before *n* or *l*, while *au* tends to show up in the middle.
