A consonant blend is two consonants side by side that each keep their own sound — you say both quickly together. In FT words, the /f/ and /t/ sounds blend at the very end, making the closing heard in *gift*, *left*, and *soft*.

The nine words in this list give children practice hearing and writing the FT blend across a range of familiar, everyday words — from short vowel patterns like *gift* and *lift* to words like *craft*, *drift*, and *shift*. Recognizing FT as a reliable final unit helps children read and spell this pattern wherever they find it.
