The TH digraph is one of the most common in English — and one of the most interesting. T and H together produce a sound that neither letter makes alone, but TH can actually make two slightly different sounds. In words like *the*, *that*, *them*, and *they*, the tongue vibrates and the sound is voiced. In words like *thing*, *think*, and *thank*, the tongue is in the same position but the sound is quieter and unvoiced. Both sounds are spelled the same way, which is part of what makes TH worth practicing carefully.

The ten words in this list cover both TH sounds and include some of the most frequently used words in English. Because so many everyday words rely on this digraph — from *the* and *this* to *then* and *think* — children who feel confident with TH spelling have a real head start in both reading and writing.
