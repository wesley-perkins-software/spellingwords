### Use known parts to hold longer words

The words *before*, *story*, *between*, and *under* can be held in spoken and written chunks. In *before*, the final **ore** is the same r-controlled spelling found in *more*. The two **ee** letters sit in the middle of *between*, and *story* ends with **y** spelling the final long e sound.

### Compare vowel spellings and endings

The words *gave* and *made* use silent **e** to mark the long a sound. *Those* also has a silent **e**, while its final **s** sounds like **z**. In *found*, the vowel sound is spelled **ou**. The notes isolate three less expected vowel spellings and distinguish *its* from the contraction *it's*.
