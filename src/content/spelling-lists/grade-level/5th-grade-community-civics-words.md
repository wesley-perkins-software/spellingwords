By fifth grade, students are reading and writing about how communities work — from a small *village* to sprawling *cities*, from a classroom *president* to the idea of *democracy* itself. These ten words appear in social studies texts, civics discussions, and everyday school life. They connect the places people live (*country*, *village*) to the roles people play (*principal*, *police*, *spectator*).

Spelling them without hesitation lets a fifth grader stay focused on the ideas behind the words.
