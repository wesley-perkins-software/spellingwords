Homophones sound alike but have different spellings and meanings. In third grade, students write longer sentences and paragraphs, so they need to choose the spelling that matches what they mean.

The words *there*, *their*, and *they're* sound alike, but each one has a different job. The same is true for *your* and *you're*, *its* and *it's*, and pairs like *right* and *write*. This list teaches students to use meaning, not just sound, when spelling.
