In GL words, the /g/ and /l/ sounds blend together at the very start — each consonant keeps its own sound, but they move into one another smoothly. You can hear it clearly in *glad*, *glass*, and *glow*.

The six words in this list give children practice with the GL blend across familiar, useful words — from everyday objects like *glass* and *glove* to describing words like *glad* and *glow*. Spotting GL as a reliable unit helps children read and spell this blend wherever they find it.
