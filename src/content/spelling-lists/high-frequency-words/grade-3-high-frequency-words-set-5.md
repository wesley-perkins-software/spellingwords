### Use full forms to spell contractions

Each contraction in this set preserves letters from two words. *I'm* comes from **I am**, *it's* comes from **it is** or **it has**, and *don't* comes from **do not**. Say the full form, keep the letters that remain, and place the apostrophe where letters were removed. The notes give extra support to the capital letter in *I'm* and the meaning-dependent choice between *it's* and *its*.

### Organize the longer spellings

The words *remember* and *during* have clear syllable anchors: **re-mem-ber** and **dur-ing**. *Instead* can be held in the stable written chunks **in-stead**, and *while* uses silent **e** to mark its long i sound. The final **y** in *study* spells a long e sound, while *such* has a familiar short vowel and final **ch**.

Three spellings need closer attention. Meaning selects *weather* rather than its homophone *whether*. In *certain*, the written ending **ain** may be less distinct in speech, and *money* uses **o** for its first vowel sound. Their notes isolate the exact letters to retrieve.
