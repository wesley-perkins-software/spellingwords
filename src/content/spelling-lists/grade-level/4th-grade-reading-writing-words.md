By fourth grade, students are expected to write structured responses, cite evidence, and discuss what an author does and why. These thirteen words are the building blocks of that work — the terms a teacher uses when giving an assignment and the terms a student needs when writing their answer.

Spelling them automatically lets a fourth grader stay focused on the thinking, not the mechanics.
