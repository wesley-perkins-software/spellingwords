Practice spelling and capitalizing all twelve months of the year. These optional vocabulary words are useful for calendars, journals, birthdays, and writing about the seasons.

Each month is a name, so begin it with a capital letter. Watch for the tricky spellings in *February* (a silent-feeling first *r*) and *August* (which does not sound the way it looks). This page is additional practice, not part of the Grade 2 Core Spelling route.
