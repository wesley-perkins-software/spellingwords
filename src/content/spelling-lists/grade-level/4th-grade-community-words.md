Fourth graders meet important vocabulary in science and social studies, but those words do not need separate spelling pages unless they form a strong instructional sequence. This list gathers useful content-area words that help students write about communities, places, people, observations, and resources.

The goal is not to memorize a subject glossary. The goal is to spell high-value academic words accurately when they appear in reading responses, reports, and classroom explanations.
