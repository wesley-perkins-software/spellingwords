From the school bus in the morning to a family road trip, these eight words cover the ways people get from place to place. *Car*, *bus*, and *train* are everyday words; *bicycle* and *subway* are a little longer and worth extra attention.

This is optional additional practice, not part of the Grade 2 Core Spelling route.
